# DataLens — Professional Report Templates Reference

## Template Architecture

DataLens provides 8 standardized, pre-built business templates designed for executive presentations, departmental reviews, and analytical deep dives. Each template defines default metadata, section ordering, KPI slots, and layout structures.

---

## 1. Executive Business Review (`exec_review`)
- **Target Audience:** C-Suite, Board of Directors, Managing Partners
- **Focus:** High-level strategic briefing covering cross-functional performance, macro drivers, top risks, and strategic next steps.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Executive Summary (Strategic overview)
  3. Key Performance Indicators (Enterprise metrics)
  4. Visual Analytics (Quarterly trend & segment contribution)
  5. Key Findings & Market Drivers
  6. Strategic Recommendations & Priorities
  7. Data Provenance & Cryptographic Audit

---

## 2. Sales Performance Report (`sales_perf`)
- **Target Audience:** VP of Sales, CRO, Sales Operations, Regional Directors
- **Focus:** Revenue targets vs. actuals, conversion funnel velocity, pipeline health, win rates, and rep attainment.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Sales Executive Summary
  3. Sales KPIs (Bookings, ARR/MRR, Win Rate, Average Deal Size)
  4. Funnel & Regional Performance Charts
  5. Pipeline Risk & Variance Factors
  6. Sales Action Plan
  7. Data Provenance & Verification

---

## 3. Financial Performance Report (`financial_perf`)
- **Target Audience:** CFO, Finance Committee, Controllers, Investment Analysts
- **Focus:** P&L summary, gross margins, EBITDA, OPEX variance against budget, cash flow metrics, and burn rate.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Financial Summary
  3. Core Financial KPIs (Revenue, Gross Margin %, EBITDA, Free Cash Flow)
  4. Expense Breakdown & Budget Variance Visuals
  5. Margin Drivers & Audit Notes
  6. Capital Allocation Recommendations
  7. Ledger & Data Provenance

---

## 4. Marketing Analytics Summary (`marketing_analytics`)
- **Target Audience:** CMO, Demand Generation Leads, Growth Marketers
- **Focus:** Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), channel attribution, campaign conversion rates, and pipeline generation.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Campaign Overview
  3. Growth KPIs (CAC, ROAS, MQLs, SQL Conversion Rate)
  4. Channel Attribution Charts
  5. Top Performing Cohorts & Insights
  6. Budget Reallocation Recommendations
  7. Data Provenance

---

## 5. Customer & Retention Analysis (`customer_analytics`)
- **Target Audience:** Chief Customer Officer, VP Customer Success, Product Leads
- **Focus:** Churn analysis, Net Retention Rate (NRR), customer lifetime value (LTV), cohort retention curves, and CSAT/NPS feedback.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Retention Summary
  3. Retention KPIs (Logo Churn %, NRR %, LTV/CAC, NPS)
  4. Cohort Heatmaps & Retention Curves
  5. Churn Drivers & Customer Feedback Analysis
  6. Retention Playbooks & Recommendations
  7. Data Provenance

---

## 6. Operations & Fulfillment Review (`ops_fulfillment`)
- **Target Audience:** COO, Supply Chain VP, Logistics & Fulfillment Leads
- **Focus:** Lead time distribution, order fulfillment rates, SLA adherence, unit processing costs, and inventory turn velocity.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Operational Summary
  3. Operational KPIs (Order Fulfillment Rate %, Lead Time, SLA %, Cost per Unit)
  4. Throughput & Bottleneck Charts
  5. Root Cause Analysis
  6. Process Optimization Recommendations
  7. Data Provenance

---

## 7. Predictive & ML Forecast Report (`predictive_analytics`)
- **Target Audience:** Chief Data Officer, Analytics Leaders, Strategy Planners
- **Focus:** Outputs from DataLens ML models (forecasting, regression, classification, clustering), feature importance rankings, confidence intervals, and model performance metrics.
- **Default Sections:**
  1. Cover Page
  2. Model Brief & Methodology
  3. Forecast Metrics (RMSE, MAPE, $R^2$, Accuracy)
  4. Forecast Visualizations & Confidence Bands
  5. Feature Importance & Key Predictors
  6. Business Application & Guidance
  7. Model Registry & Data Provenance

---

## 8. Custom Modular Report (`modular_custom`)
- **Target Audience:** Cross-functional project teams and ad-hoc analysis
- **Focus:** Flexible canvas allowing arbitrary combinations of KPI metrics, bound charts, tabular breakdowns, and custom narrative notes.
- **Default Sections:** User-selected from all available section types with custom drag-and-drop or ordered positioning.
